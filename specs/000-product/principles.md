# NOMAD Ops — Product Principles

**Product:** NOMAD Ops
**Organization:** Asteria Manufacturing Group
**Spec:** 000-product
**Status:** APPROVED

---

## Principles

1. **Useful before impressive.** The product must solve clear operational needs rather than use AI for its own sake.
2. **Evidence before confidence.** Answers about company operations must show their supporting source or state clearly when evidence is insufficient.
3. **Permission before access.** A user may only see or act on information within their approved role, department, site and task.
4. **Human control for sensitive actions.** AI may recommend or prepare an action, but it cannot approve its own sensitive action.
5. **Simple when possible.** Search, rules or direct retrieval should be used when they solve the need more reliably than AI.
6. **Honest outcomes.** Failure, partial completion and uncertainty must remain visible.
7. **Safe simulation, real discipline.** Business systems and data may be fictional, but security and operational controls must be realistic.
8. **One coherent fictional company.** Synthetic records must describe the same people, sites, assets and events across every simulated system.
9. **Privacy by design.** Use the minimum data necessary and never use real sensitive information when synthetic data is sufficient.
10. **Traceable activity.** Important access, decisions, approvals and actions must be reviewable afterward.
11. **No silent expansion.** New functions, users, data or integrations require an approved specification.
