# NOMAD Ops — Product Success Criteria

**Product:** NOMAD Ops
**Organization:** Asteria Manufacturing Group
**Spec:** 000-product
**Status:** APPROVED

---

## First-Version Success

The first version is successful when all of the following are demonstrated:

1. Each initial demonstration journey can be completed from beginning to end.
2. Users can find the correct authorized information and see where it came from.
3. Users are prevented from viewing information outside their approved role, department or site.
4. Sensitive actions cannot run without the correct human approval.
5. A rejected or expired approval does not execute the requested action.
6. Completed actions have evidence of their result.
7. Failed, partial or uncertain actions are clearly reported and are not presented as successful.
8. Important decisions and actions can be reviewed through an audit history.
9. No critical secret or prohibited data appears in AI input, search indexes, user responses or operational records.
10. The synthetic dataset contains no real personal or confidential business data.
11. Shared fictional entities and events remain consistent across all simulated systems used by a journey.
12. The product continues to provide non-AI functions when an AI provider is unavailable, wherever the journey allows it.
13. Every implemented journey is checked against its approved acceptance criteria.

## Evidence Required

Success must be supported by repeatable demonstrations and tests, including:

- permitted-access examples;
- denied-access examples;
- approval and rejection examples;
- successful, failed and uncertain execution examples;
- cross-system synthetic-data consistency checks;
- source and audit-history checks;
- checks that protected information is not exposed.

Numerical performance, capacity, availability and recovery targets remain outside this proposal until expected usage and deployment needs are approved.
