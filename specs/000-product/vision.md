# NOMAD Ops — Product Vision

**Product:** NOMAD Ops
**Organization:** Asteria Manufacturing Group
**Spec:** 000-product
**Status:** APPROVED

---

## Vision

NOMAD Ops is a secure internal operations assistant for the fictional Asteria Manufacturing Group.

It helps authorized employees find reliable business information, understand operational situations and carry out controlled tasks across company systems from one place.

The product must demonstrate how AI, search, automation and human approval can work together in a realistic enterprise environment without allowing AI to decide permissions or act outside approved limits.

## Intended Value

NOMAD Ops should help Asteria users:

- find relevant information faster;
- connect facts held in different business systems;
- understand incidents and alerts with supporting evidence;
- receive practical recommendations;
- carry out approved actions with clear human control;
- see what happened, why it happened and who approved it.

## Demonstration Environment

Asteria, its people and its business records are fictional.

The demonstration will use synthetic but coherent data shared across simulated enterprise systems. The same employees, sites, departments, equipment, suppliers, documents and incidents must remain consistently related wherever they appear.

The platform controls, audit trail, permissions and failure handling must behave as they would in a real organization.

## Vision Boundary

NOMAD Ops supports existing business systems; it does not replace them. It must use the simplest approved method for each task and use AI only where it adds clear value.
